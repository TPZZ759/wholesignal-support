WholeSignal

Privacy Policy

WholeSignal keeps your data on your iPhone and Apple Watch. We don't collect it, see it or sell it.

Effective date: 9 October 2026 · Applies to WholeSignal 2.0 for iPhone and Apple Watch

The short version.

  • There is no account, no sign-in and no WholeSignal server. The app makes no internet connections. Its links to this page and the support page open in Safari.
  • WholeSignal is free, with no purchases, so there are no payment details either.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting tools and no third-party code that collects data.
  • Your workouts go to Apple Health. Everything else stays in the app's own storage on your devices.
  • Health data is never used for advertising, never shared with anyone and never stored in iCloud by WholeSignal.
  • You can erase everything WholeSignal keeps on your iPhone from Settings, in one step.

Who we are

WholeSignal is published by Vithuja Padukka ("we"). For questions about this policy, write to WholeSignal support on GitHub.

WholeSignal is free and available in every country and region where the App Store offers it. This policy applies to everyone who uses it, wherever they are. Because the app collects nothing, there is no personal data on our side to access, correct, export or delete, under any country's privacy law.

What WholeSignal stores, and where

All of this stays on your devices. We never receive any of it.

On your iPhone, in the app's private storage

DataPurpose and protection

The items you choose to track (it starts with a few everyday ones, such as water and vitamins, and you can add any of your own), your entries, schedules, reminder settings and supply records. The log file uses iOS's strongest file protection, so it can't be read while your iPhone is locked.

SettingsYour answers from setup (whether you use an Apple Watch, your bike type, the kettlebells you own and their unit, whether you have a pull-up bar, and whether the Log tab shows), heart-rate zones, sleep settings, bike setup, the Bluetooth sensors you picked, lock and discreet-mode settings, and the aliases you type for discreet mode. If you add WholeSignal's Focus filter to your Sleep Focus, it also stores the times Sleep Focus turned on, to estimate when your lights went out.

On your Apple Watch

  • Today's session and the recovery, load and sleep ring values, so the Watch and its complications can show them. These are kept in an App Group shared only by the WholeSignal Watch app and its complication. Nothing from your log is stored there. The quick-log complication reads only an on/off flag for discreet mode.
  • A copy of your log items (or their aliases in discreet mode), today's and tomorrow's scheduled doses, and entries you make on the Watch until your iPhone confirms them. This is what makes quick log work on the Watch.

Between iPhone and Apple Watch

The two devices sync using Apple's WatchConnectivity, which talks directly to your paired Watch. WholeSignal sends nothing through any server of its own. Live workout data moves between iPhone and Watch through Apple Health's workout mirroring.

In Apple Health

With your permission, WholeSignal saves the workouts you start and finish, with their heart rate, active energy, cycling cadence, cycling power and the effort rating you give. Those records then belong to the Health app, where you can view or delete them.

Backups

WholeSignal doesn't use iCloud Drive, CloudKit or iCloud key-value storage, and it never uploads anything. Ride recordings, ride summaries, effort ratings and progression levels are excluded from iPhone backups. If you back up your iPhone (to iCloud or a computer), iOS includes the app's other files, your log and settings among them, as it does for every app. Apple controls those backups, and you can protect them with a strong passcode or Advanced Data Protection.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

With your permission, WholeSignal reads these Health types: workouts, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, active energy, VO2 max, cycling cadence, cycling power, heart-rate recovery, respiratory rate, sleeping wrist temperature, blood oxygen, sleep, and your date of birth (used to estimate heart-rate zones and sleep need). It writes workouts, heart rate, active energy, cycling cadence, cycling power and workout effort.

Health data is used only to give you training, recovery and sleep features inside the app.

  • It is never used for advertising or marketing, and never used to build a profile of you.
  • It is never shared with, sold to or disclosed to third parties, including data brokers.
  • It is never stored in iCloud by WholeSignal and never leaves your devices through the app.

You can change or withdraw access at any time in the Health app: open your profile, tap Apps, then WholeSignal (or go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Health).

What we never collect

  • Your name, email, phone number or any account details. There is no account.
  • Location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers or anything else used for tracking. WholeSignal doesn't track you across apps or websites.
  • Usage analytics, diagnostics or crash logs. If you choose to share crash reports with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may pass anonymous crash reports to us under Apple's own terms.
  • Any content from your log. We have no way to see it.

Face ID and passcode

You can lock the Log tab, or the whole app, with Face ID. iOS does the check, and WholeSignal only learns whether it succeeded. Your face data never reaches the app. If Face ID isn't available, iOS asks for your device passcode, which WholeSignal also never sees. The lock is off until you turn it on.

Notifications

WholeSignal uses only local notifications, which your iPhone schedules for itself. It doesn't use push notifications or any notification server. It asks for permission only when you first turn on a reminder. Reminders cover log routines (including "1 day late" notices), supply and discard-by notices, Daily Hybrid training days, and an optional sleep wind-down reminder. Log reminders don't name an item unless you turn names on. Widgets, complications and the Lock Screen never show log entries. To schedule reminders while the app is closed, WholeSignal uses Background App Refresh, which runs on the device only.

Bluetooth sensors

If you allow Bluetooth, WholeSignal connects only to the bike sensors you choose: heart-rate straps, power meters, speed and cadence sensors, and smart bikes or trainers. It remembers those sensors' identifiers so it can reconnect to them. Readings appear on screen during a ride, are kept in the ride's workout file, and can be saved to Apple Health with the workout. With a smart bike, WholeSignal can send resistance or power targets to it. It doesn't scan for or record any other devices.

Live Activity and complications

During a workout, the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island show the session's progress and heart rate. Watch complications show today's suggested session, and a shortcut to quick log that shows no names, counts or times. None of these ever show log content.

Children

WholeSignal is not directed at children and is not meant for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don't knowingly collect data from anyone, children included, because the app collects no data at all.

Your choices and deleting your data

  • Everything WholeSignal stores on your iPhone: in WholeSignal, open Settings, then Your data, then Erase all WholeSignal data. This removes your log, supplies and routines, ride recordings and summaries, effort ratings, progression levels, cached Health readings, reminders and settings. You confirm twice, and with Face ID or your passcode if you use the lock. Your Apple Watch's copy of the log is replaced the next time it syncs; to clear the Watch completely, delete WholeSignal from it. We hold no copy anywhere, so this can't be undone.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone (and your Apple Watch) also removes its files and settings from the device.
  • Health records: erasing or deleting WholeSignal doesn't change Apple Health. Delete the workouts it saved, or turn off its access, in the Health app.
  • Permissions: turn Health, Bluetooth, notifications or Face ID access on or off in iOS Settings at any time.

We don't hold your data, so we have nothing to send you, correct or delete on our side. If you contact us through GitHub, your request is public on GitHub and covered by GitHub's own privacy terms; we only use it to reply. Please don't include health details.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, we'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released. If WholeSignal ever starts sending data off your devices, we'll explain that here first, and in the App Store privacy details.
